## The rise of LLMs in the AI landscape

### Natural Language Processing (NLP)
technology that allows computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language in a meaningful way

### Large Language Model
Is a subset of NLP that generate human-like text responses, making them integral to many areas of your life, from customer service to personalized education and entertainment.
* **Large:** Training data and computer power
* **Language:** Human-like text
* **Models:** Learn complex patterns using text data

### AI chatbots
Two main categories

#### General-Purpose Assistants
answer prompts, write code, or search the web
* ChatGPT
* Gemini

#### Business/Industry Bots
automate customer service, sales, and travel bookings
* Sephora
* Bank of America's Erica

### Applications
* Sentiment analysis
* Identifying themes
* Translating text or speech
* Generating code
* Next-word prediction
